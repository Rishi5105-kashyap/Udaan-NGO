Our Story & Vision

Why we started: Udaan was born from a simple but profound belief: when you empower a woman, you empower an entire generation. We saw the vast potential in women that remained untapped due to lack of resources and support.

Our Mission: Our NGO works to support women through education, skill development, positive thinking, and self-confidence. We provide the stepping stones for women to achieve financial independence and social dignity.

We believe every woman has a unique flight to take. We are just the wind beneath her wings.

Our Core Programs

Skill Training

Vocational training in tech, crafts, and business management to ensure sustainable livelihoods.

Education Support

Scholarships and resources for girls and women pursuing higher education and basic literacy.

Health Awareness

Workshops on mental health, reproductive rights, and general wellness.

Career Guidance

Mentorship programs connecting ambitious women with industry leaders.

Self-Help Groups

Fostering micro-communities where women save, invest, and support each other.

Motivation Sessions

Regular seminars focusing on self-esteem, leadership, and breaking barriers.
